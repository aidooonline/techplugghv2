Where each one wins

On raw CPU score, the Ryzen 5 3500U edges the 8th-generation Core i5, and its integrated Radeon Vega graphics are noticeably stronger than Intel UHD 620 for light photo and video work. So for pure performance per cedi, Ryzen has a small lead. Intel's advantage is not really about speed here: it is the EliteBook itself. The Core i5 lives in HP's premium EliteBook chassis, which is more widely available on the used market, tends to hold resale value better, and is the platform most people already trust for business.

For everyday office work, browsing, video calls and study, you will not feel the difference between them. Both have four or more cores and pair with a fast SSD, which is the part that actually makes a laptop feel quick. The SSD matters more than the Intel-versus-AMD choice; see 8GB vs 16GB RAM for the other upgrade that makes a real difference.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Intel Core i5 EliteBook if you want the premium build, the widest choice and the best resale. Buy the Ryzen 5 ProBook if you want the most processing power for the money and you do light creative work where the Vega graphics help. Neither is a wrong choice. Frequently asked questions

Is Ryzen 5 better than Core i5 for a business laptop?

On raw benchmarks, the Ryzen 5 3500U scores a little higher than the 8th-generation Core i5, and its graphics are stronger. In everyday office work the two feel the same, and the Intel EliteBook has the more premium build and better resale.

Does the Intel or AMD choice affect battery life?

Not meaningfully for these business models. Both deliver a full working day of office tasks. Battery condition on a used unit matters far more than the chip brand.

Which is better for light photo or video editing?

The Ryzen 5, because its Radeon Vega integrated graphics are stronger than Intel UHD 620 for light creative work.

Does Intel or AMD matter more than the SSD or RAM?

No. A fast SSD and enough RAM make the biggest difference to how quick a laptop feels. Both an i5 EliteBook and a Ryzen 5 ProBook ship with an SSD.

Which holds resale value better?

The Intel Core i5 EliteBook generally holds resale value better, thanks to HP's premium build and strong brand recognition on the used market.
