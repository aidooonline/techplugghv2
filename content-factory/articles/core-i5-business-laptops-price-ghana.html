What a Core i5 actually delivers

The 8th-generation Core i5 in these EliteBooks has four cores and eight threads, a base clock of 1.6 GHz and turbo up to about 3.9 to 4.1 GHz. On PassMark it scores roughly 6,000 to 6,200, which places it in the upper-middle of all laptop processors ever tested. Paired with the NVMe SSD, the laptop boots to the Windows desktop in under 20 seconds and opens apps almost instantly. In practice it handles Windows 11, the full Microsoft Office suite, 15 to 25 browser tabs, Zoom and Teams calls, WhatsApp and email, VS Code and web development, and accounting software such as QuickBooks or Tally without strain.
