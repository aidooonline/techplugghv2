What you are getting

The 840 G6 runs an 8th-generation Whiskey Lake Core i5, most commonly the i5-8265U or i5-8365U. Both are quad-core, eight-thread chips that boost to around 3.9GHz and land near a PassMark CPU score of 6,000 to 6,200. In plain terms, that is more than enough for Microsoft Office, dozens of browser tabs, video calls, accounting software and light photo work at the same time. The 256GB NVMe SSD is the part you feel most: it boots Windows in under 20 seconds and makes the whole machine feel quick.

The screen is a 14-inch Full HD (1920 x 1080) IPS panel. Standard units are rated around 250 nits, which is comfortable indoors and in most offices; brighter SureView privacy panels exist but are less common on the used market. Weight is about 1.48kg, and the 50Wh battery comfortably covers a working day of office tasks. RAM ships as 8GB, 16GB or 32GB depending on the unit, and the platform supports up to 32GB, so an 8GB unit can be upgraded later.
