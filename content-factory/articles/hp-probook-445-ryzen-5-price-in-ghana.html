The Ryzen 5 3500U in the ProBook 445r G6 has 4 cores and 8 threads and records a PassMark CPU Mark of about 7,800, roughly 12 to 18 percent faster in multi-threaded work than the 8th-gen Intel Core i5 found in the EliteBook 830 and 840 models.
