Quick answer: The HP EliteBook 830 G6 and 840 G6 are the same laptop in two sizes. Both run an 8th-generation quad-core Core i5 (PassMark around 6,000 to 6,200), a 256GB NVMe SSD and the same aluminium build. The difference is the screen: the 830 G6 has a compact 13.3-inch display and is lighter (around 1.33kg), while the 840 G6 has a roomier 14-inch display (around 1.48kg). Choose the 830 G6 for portability, the 840 G6 for a bigger workspace. Both sell from GHS 2,500 to 2,600, UK-used and tested.

The only real difference is size

Internally these two are the same machine: the same 8th-generation Core i5 family, the same NVMe SSD, the same keyboard and the same business-grade security. Performance is identical. The decision comes down to how you work. The 830 G6 is a 13.3-inch ultrabook that is noticeably lighter and easier to carry all day, which makes it a favourite for students and people who move around. The 840 G6 gives you a 14-inch screen with a little more room for spreadsheets and split-screen work, at a small weight penalty.

Which should you buy?

Pick the 830 G6 if portability matters most, for example if you commute across Accra or travel often. Pick the 840 G6 if you spend all day in front of the screen and want more space. If you want the same 14-inch experience for less, also look at the 840 G5. For memory guidance, see 8GB vs 16GB RAM.

Bottom line

The 830 G6 and 840 G6 perform the same, so choose by screen size. Lighter and 13.3-inch, or roomier and 14-inch. Both are UK-used, tested and graded, from GHS 2,500 to 2,600.
