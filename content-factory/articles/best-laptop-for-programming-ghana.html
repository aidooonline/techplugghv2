What matters for a programming laptop

For development, the things that matter most are RAM (for running your editor, browser, database and containers at once), a fast SSD (for builds, installs and Git operations), and a capable multi-core CPU. A comfortable keyboard and a good screen matter too, since you look at both all day. The EliteBook line delivers on all of these: an SSD as standard, a solid multi-core Core i5, an excellent keyboard, and a Full HD IPS screen.
