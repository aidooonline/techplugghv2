Why RAM matters (and why the SSD matters more)

RAM is your laptop's short-term working memory. The more you have, the more apps and tabs you can keep active at once without slowdown. But for most everyday users, the biggest speed jump does not come from RAM at all: it comes from the SSD. Every UK-used EliteBook here already has a fast NVMe SSD, which is why even the 8GB models boot in under 20 seconds and feel quick. RAM becomes the deciding factor only once you push into heavy multitasking or specialised work.

Who should choose 8GB

Choose 8GB if you are a student, an office worker, a business owner doing accounting and email, or anyone whose day is Office, browsing, video calls and WhatsApp. This covers the large majority of buyers, and it is why most of the value-band EliteBooks ship with 8GB. The EliteBook 840 G6 8GB from GHS 2,600 is a strong pick.

Who should choose 16GB

Choose 16GB if you run virtual machines or Docker, keep 40 or more browser tabs open, work with large datasets or spreadsheets, do serious photo or video editing, or want a machine that stays comfortable for years as software gets heavier. The EliteBook 840 G5 16GB from GHS 2,750 and the 840 G6 16GB from GHS 2,850 are the value options.

Can you upgrade RAM later?

On many of these EliteBooks, yes. The 830 G6 and 840 G6, for example, ship with a single 8GB module and a free memory slot, and are officially expandable to 32GB. That means you can start with 8GB and add more later if your needs grow. If you already know you need the headroom, though, buying a 16GB unit up front is simpler and often cheaper than sourcing a module separately.

Bottom line

8GB is enough for most people and keeps the price lowest. 16GB is the right call for heavy multitasking, development, or long-term headroom, and on these EliteBooks it costs only about GHS 250 more. Frequently asked questions

Is 8GB RAM enough for a laptop in 2026?

Yes for office work, study, browsing and video calls. With an SSD, an 8GB EliteBook runs Windows 11 and Office smoothly. Choose 16GB for heavy multitasking, virtual machines or large datasets.

Do I need 16GB RAM for programming?

For web development and everyday coding, 8GB works, but 16GB is more comfortable if you run virtual machines, Docker or multiple heavy tools at once.

How much more does 16GB cost on these laptops?

About GHS 250 more. 8GB EliteBooks start from GHS 2,500 and 16GB from GHS 2,750, as of July 2026.

Can I add more RAM to an EliteBook later?

On models like the 830 G6 and 840 G6, yes. They have a free memory slot and are officially expandable to 32GB.

Does more RAM make a laptop faster?

More RAM helps when you run many apps at once. For general speed, the SSD matters most, and every EliteBook here already has one.
