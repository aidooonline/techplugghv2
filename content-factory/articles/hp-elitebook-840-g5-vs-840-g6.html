Quick answer: In everyday use the HP EliteBook 840 G5 and 840 G6 feel nearly identical. Both are 14-inch Full HD business laptops with an 8th-generation quad-core Core i5, a 256GB NVMe SSD, a 50Wh battery and a 1.48kg aluminium body. The G6 uses slightly newer i5 chips (i5-8265U or 8365U, PassMark around 6,000 to 6,200) versus the G5 (i5-8250U or 8350U, around 5,900 to 6,100), and offers brighter screen options. The practical verdict in Ghana: buy the G5 to save money, or the G6 for the newest generation and a brighter panel. Both sell from GHS 2,500.

What actually differs

The gap between these two generations is small. The G6 uses the marginally faster Whiskey Lake refresh of the same Core i5 family, so on paper it scores a little higher, but the difference is in the low single digits of percent and you will not notice it in Office, browsing or video calls. The more useful G6 advantage is screen brightness: it offers brighter panel options that help outdoors and near windows, while some G5 panels are rated closer to 220 nits. Otherwise the chassis, keyboard, ports and SSD are the same story.

Which should you buy?

Buy the 840 G5 if you want the same real-world experience for the lowest price. Buy the 840 G6 if you want the newest generation and a brighter screen. For a lighter 13-inch option, compare the 830 G6. Whichever you choose, pick a 16GB unit if you do programming or heavy multitasking; see 8GB vs 16GB RAM.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 840 G6 much faster than the 840 G5?

No. The G6 uses a slightly newer 8th-generation Core i5, so it scores a few percent higher on benchmarks, but in everyday tasks the two feel the same.

What is the main difference between the 840 G5 and 840 G6?

Mostly the processor generation and screen brightness. The G6 has newer i5 chips and brighter panel options; the G5 usually costs less for the same experience.

Which is better value in Ghana?

The 840 G5 is usually the better value because it delivers nearly the same performance for less. The G6 is worth the small premium if you want the newest generation or a brighter screen.

Do they use the same battery and charger?

Both use a 50Wh battery and the same USB-C charging standard, and both last a full working day of office tasks.

Can both be upgraded to 16GB or 32GB?

Yes. Both support up to 32GB of RAM, so an 8GB unit of either can be upgraded later.
