Performance in real use

The Core i5 in the 840 G6 records a PassMark CPU Mark of about 6,030 to 6,216 with a Geekbench 5 multi-core result near 2,800, the same silicon as the smaller 830 G6. With four cores, eight threads and a fast NVMe SSD, it handles Windows 11, the full Microsoft Office suite, Zoom and Teams calls, 15 to 25 browser tabs, accounting packages such as QuickBooks and Tally, VS Code and web development, and 1080p video without strain. The SSD boots Windows in under 20 seconds.

Choosing a RAM size: 8GB is comfortable for everyday office work and browsing. Step up to the 16GB model if you keep dozens of tabs open, run virtual machines, or work in heavier design and development tools. The 32GB unit is a rare single-stock configuration aimed at developers and analysts with memory-hungry workloads. The 840 G6 is not intended for AAA gaming or 4K video rendering; for that, see the HP ZBook 15 G5 workstation.

Why choose the 14-inch 840 G6

The 840 G6 uses the exact same processor as the 13.3-inch 830 G6 at the same starting price, but gives you a larger 14-inch screen and a full-size keyboard with a numeric-friendly layout. If you work in spreadsheets, keep several windows side by side, or simply prefer a bigger display, the 840 G6 is the natural pick. If maximum portability matters more, the 830 G6 is lighter. Both are 8th-generation EliteBooks with the same build quality and security.
