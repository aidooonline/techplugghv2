HP EliteBook: premium build, best value here

The EliteBook is the one that looks and feels expensive. The 840 G5 and G6 use a brushed aluminium chassis that is thin (around 17.9mm) and light (about 1.48kg), with a crisp backlit keyboard and clever spill-drainage channels under the keys. On the used market in Ghana it also happens to be widely available and keenly priced, which is why it is our core stock. The trade-off some reviewers note is that EliteBook batteries can degrade a little faster than a Latitude's, so buying a tested unit matters.

Dell Latitude: battery champion, easiest to repair

The Latitude is the workhorse. It is a touch heavier and more utilitarian than the EliteBook, but it is famous for long real-world battery life and batteries that hold up well after years of use. The 5490 and later models add a pointing stick with dedicated mouse buttons, which spreadsheet users love, and Dell has the widest spare-parts ecosystem, so repairs are simple. If all-day battery matters most, the Latitude is a strong choice.

Lenovo ThinkPad: the keyboard king, but hard to find used

The ThinkPad has the best keyboard of the three, full stop: deep, tactile key travel and the signature red TrackPoint nub that lets you move the cursor without leaving the home row. It is also built like a tank to military durability standards. The catch in 2026 is supply: good used ThinkPads are in high demand and disappear from the used market quickly, so they are the hardest of the three to buy tested and graded at a fair price.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best business laptop brand: HP, Dell or Lenovo?

All three make excellent business lines. HP EliteBook leads on premium build and value, Dell Latitude on battery life and repairability, and Lenovo ThinkPad on keyboard feel and durability. The best choice depends on your priority and what is available used.

Which has the best keyboard?

The Lenovo ThinkPad, with deep key travel and the TrackPoint nub. The HP EliteBook keyboard is crisp and backlit, and the Dell Latitude keyboard is excellent too, with a pointing stick on newer models.

Which has the best battery life?

The Dell Latitude generally leads, with strong real-world battery life that holds up over years. EliteBooks and ThinkPads are good but their batteries can fade a little faster.

Why are used ThinkPads hard to find?

ThinkPads are in high demand on the used market and sell out quickly, so tested, fairly priced units are scarce compared with EliteBooks and Latitudes.
