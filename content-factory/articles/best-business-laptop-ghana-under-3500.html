Best overall: HP EliteBook 840 G6

The EliteBook 840 G6 hits the best balance under GHS 3,500. You get a 14-inch Full HD IPS screen, an 8th-generation Core i5 (PassMark around 6,000), 8GB RAM, a 256GB NVMe SSD, an aluminium chassis and business security. It runs Windows 11, Office, heavy browsing, video calls and accounting software comfortably, and it is the size most people find most usable for daily work.

Best for portability: HP EliteBook 830 G6

At 13.3 inches and about 1.33kg, the EliteBook 830 G6 is the easiest to carry all day, which makes it a favourite for students and people who commute across Accra. It uses the same processor and SSD as the 840 G6 in a smaller, lighter body for the same price.

Best value: HP EliteBook 840 G5

The EliteBook 840 G5 from GHS 2,500 is the lowest-cost way into a genuine business laptop. It is a generation older than the G6 but delivers the same everyday experience: Core i5, 256GB SSD, 14-inch screen and metal build. If budget is the priority, this is the pick.

Best for heavy work: HP ZBook 15 G5

If you do CAD, 3D, video editing or heavy development, the ZBook 15 G5 at GHS 3,500 is the machine. It pairs a 6-core Core i7 (PassMark around 11,000 to 12,000) with professional Quadro graphics, 16GB RAM and a 512GB SSD. It is heavier and pricier than the EliteBooks, but it is built for workloads they are not.

How to choose

Start with what you do. Office, study and business point to a Core i5 EliteBook, and then screen size and budget decide between the 830 G6, 840 G6 and 840 G5. Heavy multitasking points to a 16GB model. Creative or engineering work points to the ZBook.
