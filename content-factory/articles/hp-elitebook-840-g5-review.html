What you are getting

Inside is an 8th-generation Core i5, usually the i5-8250U or i5-8350U. These are quad-core, eight-thread chips landing around a PassMark score of 5,900 to 6,100, only marginally below the G6. The 256GB NVMe SSD is what makes the machine feel fast day to day, booting Windows in well under 20 seconds. The 14-inch Full HD IPS panel is rated around 220 to 250 nits depending on the unit, comfortable for indoor work. It weighs about 1.48kg and the 50Wh battery covers a normal working day. RAM comes as 8GB, 16GB or 32GB, and the platform supports up to 32GB.
